Exam Master keeps you informed at every step of your learning journey through targeted push notifications. Whether it's a live class about to start, a new test assigned by your teacher, or a payment coming due, you'll receive a timely alert so nothing catches you off guard. You can control exactly which notifications you receive at any time from your account settings.

## Types of Notifications

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Class Reminders">
    You'll receive a push notification **15 minutes before** every scheduled live session in any of your enrolled batches. The notification includes the class subject, teacher name, and a one-tap **Join Now** button so you can enter the session directly without opening the app first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Test Reminders">
    Exam Master notifies you in two situations: when a teacher **assigns a new test** to your batch, and when a test deadline is **approaching** (typically 24 hours and 1 hour before the cutoff). This gives you enough lead time to prepare and enough urgency to avoid missing the window.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Assignment Alerts">
    You'll be notified whenever a **new assignment is posted** to one of your batches and again when your teacher has **reviewed your submission** and published feedback or a grade. Tap the notification to go straight to the assignment details or your graded work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Announcements">
    Important messages from your teachers or institution — such as schedule changes, exam timetables, or holiday notices — are delivered as high-priority notifications. These appear at the top of your notification tray to ensure they don't get buried.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fee Reminders">
    When a fee payment is coming due, Exam Master sends you an advance reminder so you have time to arrange payment. You may receive reminders at the 7-day mark and again on the due date itself, depending on your institution's settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Results">
    Once your teacher publishes results for a test or evaluates an assignment, you'll receive an instant notification. Tap it to open your detailed result page, where you can review your score, see which questions you got wrong, and read any written feedback.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Managing Notification Preferences

You can turn individual notification categories on or off at any time. Changes take effect immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Tap your profile picture or the **Account** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open your account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Notification Settings">
    Scroll down and tap **Notification Settings**. You'll see a list of every notification category available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Categories On or Off">
    Use the toggle switch next to each category — such as Class Reminders, Test Reminders, or Fee Reminders — to enable or disable it. Toggling a category off means you will no longer receive push notifications for that type of event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Preferences">
    Your changes are saved automatically as you toggle each switch. You don't need to tap a separate Save button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enabling Push Notifications on Android

If you accidentally denied notification permissions when you first installed Exam Master on an Android device, follow these steps to re-enable them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Device Settings">
    Go to your phone's **Settings** app and tap **Apps** (or **Application Manager**, depending on your device).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Exam Master">
    Scroll through the list and tap **Exam Master**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Notifications">
    Tap **Notifications** and make sure **Allow Notifications** is turned on. You can also enable or disable specific notification channels — such as Class Reminders or Announcements — individually from this screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enabling Push Notifications on iOS

If you denied notification permissions on an iPhone or iPad, follow these steps to restore them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Device Settings">
    Go to the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Exam Master">
    Scroll down the settings list and tap **Exam Master**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Notifications">
    Tap **Notifications**, then toggle **Allow Notifications** on. You can also choose your preferred alert style — Lock Screen, Notification Centre, or Banners — from this screen to control how alerts appear on your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you turn off notifications at the device level, Exam Master cannot deliver any alerts — including time-sensitive class reminders — regardless of your in-app settings. Always ensure device-level permissions are enabled.
</Warning>
