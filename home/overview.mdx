The home screen is the first thing you see after logging in. It's designed to surface the most relevant information — your next class, pending assignments, recent test results — so you can get started without navigating through menus.

## What's on the Home Screen

Every element on the home screen is chosen to save you time. At a glance you can see what's coming up, what needs your attention, and how you've been performing — all without leaving the main view.

## Navigating from the Home Screen

Each element on the home screen is a direct shortcut into the relevant area of the app. Tapping any card or widget takes you exactly where you need to go without extra steps.

- **Upcoming Classes card** → Opens the class details page, where you can join the session or access recorded materials.
- **Today's Schedule timeline** → Expands into the full Schedule view for the current day and the rest of the week.
- **Quick Actions shortcuts** → Route you directly to the active class room, the test-taking screen, or your course material library.
- **Announcements widget** → Opens the full Announcements feed, where you can read, search, and archive notices.
- **Recent Activity feed** → Takes you to your detailed Results & Activity log with score breakdowns and submission history.
- **Pending Tasks list** → Links to the Assignments or Tests screen filtered to show only incomplete items.

## Personalizing Your View

You don't need to configure the home screen manually — it updates automatically based on the batches you're enrolled in and your upcoming deadlines. As you join new batches, complete tests, or receive new assignments, the relevant cards and widgets appear or update in real time. If you're enrolled in multiple batches, content from all of them is blended into a single unified view, prioritized by urgency and schedule.

<Tip>
  Tap and hold any class card on the home screen to add it to your device calendar, so you get a native reminder even when the app is closed.
</Tip>
