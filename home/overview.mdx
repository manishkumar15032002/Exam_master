The home screen is the first thing you see after logging in to Exam Master. It's designed to surface the most relevant information — your next class, pending assignments, recent test results, and important announcements — so you can get started immediately without navigating through menus.

## What's on the Home Screen

Every element on the home screen is chosen to save you time. At a glance you can see what's coming up, what needs your attention, and how you've been performing — all without leaving the main view.

## Navigating from the Home Screen

Each element on the home screen is a direct shortcut into the relevant area of the app. Tapping any card or widget takes you exactly where you need to go without extra steps.

- **Upcoming Classes card** → Opens the class details page, where you can join the session or access recorded materials.
- **Today's Schedule timeline** → Expands into the full Schedule view for the current day and the rest of the week.
- **Quick Actions shortcuts** → Route you directly to the active class room, the test-taking screen, or your course material library.
- **Announcements widget** → Opens the full Announcements feed, where you can read, search, and archive notices.
- **Recent Activity feed** → Takes you to your detailed Results & Activity log with score breakdowns and submission history.
- **Pending Tasks list** → Links to the Assignments or Tests screen filtered to show only incomplete items.

## Quick Actions Explained

The Quick Actions bar sits near the top of the home screen for immediate access. Each shortcut is context-aware and routes you to the most relevant destination based on your current schedule.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Join Class">
    Tapping **Join Class** takes you directly into your currently active or next-starting live session. If no live class is imminent, it opens the full class schedule so you can browse upcoming sessions. You'll see the subject name, teacher, and a live countdown so you know exactly when to tap.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Take Test">
    Tapping **Take Test** opens a list of all tests currently assigned to your enrolled batches, sorted by deadline. Tests that are due soonest appear at the top. Tap any test in the list to open the instructions page and begin when you're ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="View Material">
    Tapping **View Material** opens your course material library, showing all study resources — PDFs, videos, notes, and practice questions — uploaded by your teachers across all your enrolled batches. Use the filters to narrow by batch or subject.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How the Home Screen Personalizes to You

You don't need to configure the home screen manually — it updates automatically based on the batches you're enrolled in and your upcoming deadlines. As you join new batches, complete tests, or receive new assignments, the relevant cards and widgets appear or update in real time.

Here's how personalization works for each section:

- **Upcoming Classes** shows the next session across all your enrolled batches, ordered by start time. If you're in multiple batches, the soonest class always appears first.
- **Today's Schedule** is assembled from every batch you belong to, so sessions from different courses all appear in a single unified timeline for the day.
- **Pending Tasks** pulls together incomplete assignments and unattempted tests from all your batches, sorted by due date so the most urgent items are always visible first.
- **Recent Activity** reflects your last five test submissions and assignment grades, regardless of which batch they belong to.
- **Announcements** aggregates notices from all your teachers and batches into one feed, ordered by publish date.

<Tip>
  Tap and hold any class card on the home screen to add it to your device calendar, so you get a native reminder even when the app is closed.
</Tip>

## Keeping Your Home Screen Up to Date

The home screen refreshes automatically each time you open the app. If you want to force an immediate update — for example, after your teacher posts a new announcement or your test result is published — pull down on the screen to trigger a manual refresh.

<Note>
  If a batch you're enrolled in shows no upcoming classes or tasks, it means your teacher hasn't scheduled any sessions or assignments yet. Check back later or reach out to your teacher directly for updates.
</Note>
