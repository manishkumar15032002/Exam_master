Your dashboard is the command centre for tracking how you're progressing through your courses. Unlike the home screen — which focuses on what's happening right now — the dashboard gives you a deeper, data-driven view of your performance over time, your attendance habits, and how far you've come in each batch you've enrolled in.

## Dashboard Sections

The dashboard is divided into four key sections, each giving you a different lens on your learning journey.

### Performance Summary

The Performance Summary card shows your average test score across all tests taken in a batch, the total number of tests you've attempted, and a trend line covering the past 30 days. A rising trend line means your scores are improving; a flat or declining line is a signal to review recent material or reach out to your teacher.

### Attendance

The Attendance section displays your live class attendance percentage for each enrolled batch. A colour-coded indicator — green for healthy attendance, amber for moderate, and red for low — lets you spot batches where you may be falling behind on live sessions. Tap the percentage to see a class-by-class attendance breakdown.

### Batch Progress

Each enrolled batch has a progress bar showing how much of the course content you've covered so far, calculated from the lessons watched, assignments submitted, and tests completed. Use this to gauge how much runway you have left before the course ends and to pace yourself accordingly.

### Leaderboard Position

Your Leaderboard Position card shows your current rank among all students in the same batch, based on cumulative test scores. It also displays the score of the student directly above you, so you know exactly how many points separate you from moving up a place.

## Reading Your Performance Summary

The score trend chart plots your test results chronologically over the past 30 days. Each data point represents a single test attempt, and the line connects them in order. To interpret the chart effectively:

- **Upward slope** — You're improving. Keep up your current revision habits.
- **Flat line** — Your scores are consistent but not growing. Consider varying your study techniques or attempting more practice tests.
- **Downward slope** — Scores are declining. Review the topics covered in your most recent tests and speak to your teacher if the trend continues.

Tap any individual data point on the chart to see the test name, date, and your exact score for that attempt.

## Switching Between Batches

If you're enrolled in more than one batch, a **batch selector** dropdown appears at the top of the dashboard. Tap it to choose which batch's data you want to view — performance summary, attendance, and progress all update instantly to reflect the selected batch. Your leaderboard position is always batch-specific and changes accordingly when you switch.

<Note>
  Dashboard data refreshes automatically whenever you open the screen. Pull down on the dashboard to force a manual refresh if you want to make sure you're seeing the very latest figures — for example, right after submitting a test.
</Note>
