Your dashboard is the command centre for tracking how you're progressing through your courses. Unlike the home screen — which focuses on what's happening right now — the dashboard gives you a deeper, data-driven view of your performance over time, your attendance habits, and how far you've come in each batch you've enrolled in.

## Dashboard Sections

The dashboard is divided into four key sections, each giving you a different lens on your learning journey.

## Performance Summary

The Performance Summary card gives you a consolidated view of your test results for the selected batch. It shows three key metrics:

- **Average Score** — Your mean percentage across every test you've submitted in this batch. This is the clearest single indicator of how well you're absorbing the material.
- **Tests Attempted** — The total count of tests you've completed so far. Comparing this against the total tests assigned tells you whether you're keeping up with the assessment schedule.
- **30-Day Trend Line** — A chart plotting each of your test scores chronologically over the past 30 days. Each data point is one test attempt, and the line connects them in submission order.

To interpret the trend line:

- **Upward slope** — Your scores are improving. Keep up your current revision habits.
- **Flat line** — Your scores are consistent but not growing. Consider varying your study techniques or attempting more practice tests.
- **Downward slope** — Scores are declining. Review the topics from your most recent tests and speak to your teacher if the trend continues.

Tap any individual data point on the chart to see the test name, date, and your exact score for that attempt.

## Attendance

The Attendance section displays your live class attendance percentage for each enrolled batch. A colour-coded indicator makes it easy to assess your standing at a glance:

- 🟢 **Green (75% and above)** — Healthy attendance. You're keeping up with your live sessions.
- 🟡 **Amber (50–74%)** — Moderate attendance. You're missing a notable portion of live classes and may be falling behind on real-time instruction.
- 🔴 **Red (below 50%)** — Low attendance. You're at risk of missing important content. Consider reviewing recorded replays and reaching out to your teacher.

Tap the attendance percentage to open a session-by-session breakdown listing every scheduled class, whether you attended, and the date of each session.

## Batch Progress

Each enrolled batch has a progress bar that shows what percentage of the course content you've covered. The percentage is calculated from three inputs:

- **Lessons watched** — Video lessons and recorded classes you've played in the material library.
- **Assignments submitted** — Written or uploaded assignments you've turned in for the batch.
- **Tests completed** — Assessed tests you've attempted, regardless of your score.

Use the progress bar to pace yourself. If you're close to a batch's end date but the bar is significantly below 100%, prioritise catching up on any unwatched lessons or unsubmitted assignments before the course closes.

## Leaderboard Position

Your Leaderboard Position card shows where you stand among all students enrolled in the same batch, ranked by cumulative test score. The card displays:

- **Your current rank** — For example, "Rank 12 of 84 students."
- **Points to next rank** — The score gap between you and the student directly above you, so you know exactly how much improvement is needed to move up one place.

<Note>
  The leaderboard only includes students who have attempted at least one test in the batch. Students who haven't submitted any tests yet do not appear in the rankings.
</Note>

## Switching Between Batches

If you're enrolled in more than one batch, a **batch selector** dropdown appears at the top of the dashboard. Tap it to choose which batch's data you want to view — your Performance Summary, Attendance, Batch Progress, and Leaderboard Position all update instantly to reflect the selected batch.

## Keeping Your Dashboard Current

Dashboard data refreshes automatically whenever you open the screen. If you want to force an immediate update — for example, right after submitting a test or attending a class — pull down on the dashboard to trigger a manual refresh.

<Tip>
  Check your dashboard after every test submission to see how your new score affects your trend line and leaderboard position in real time.
</Tip>
