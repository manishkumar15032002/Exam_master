Stuck on a problem? Exam Master's doubt resolution feature lets you upload a photo or screenshot of any question and get a clear, detailed answer from your teacher — without waiting for the next class. It's the fastest way to unblock yourself and keep your studies moving forward.

## Submitting a Doubt

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Ask Doubt">
    Tap **Ask Doubt** from the home screen or the Learning section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the subject and topic">
    Choose the subject and topic your doubt is related to so it gets routed to the right teacher.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your question">
    Tap the **camera icon** to take a photo of your question, or tap the **gallery icon** to upload an existing screenshot from your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description (optional)">
    Add a short description to give your teacher more context about what you've tried or what's confusing you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your doubt">
    Tap **Submit** — your doubt is immediately routed to the relevant teacher for review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Make sure your photo is well-lit and the text is clearly legible to get a faster, more accurate response from your teacher.
</Tip>

## Tracking Your Doubts

You can check the status of all your submitted doubts at any time from the **My Doubts** tab. Each doubt entry shows:

- **Status** — whether your doubt is **Pending** (awaiting a response) or **Answered**.
- **Teacher's Response** — the full explanation provided by your teacher, available once the doubt is answered.
- **Timestamp** — when you submitted the doubt and when it was answered.

## Getting a Response

When your teacher answers your doubt, you'll receive a **push notification** on your device. Tap the notification to open the response directly. You can also find the answered doubt in the **My Doubts** tab at any time.

<Note>
  Doubt response times depend on your institution's schedule. Most doubts are answered within **24 hours**.
</Note>
