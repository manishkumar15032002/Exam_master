Recorded classes give you the flexibility to learn on your own schedule. Whether you missed a live session or want to revisit a difficult concept, every recorded class is available to watch, pause, rewind, and replay as many times as you need.

## Finding Recorded Classes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Recorded Classes">
    From the home screen, tap **Recorded Classes** in the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your batch and subject">
    Choose the relevant batch and subject from the list to browse available sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a session">
    Tap any session from the list to open it and begin playback.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Playback Controls

While watching a recorded class, you have full control over your viewing experience:

- **Play/Pause**: Tap the screen or use the play/pause button to control playback.
- **Seek Bar**: Drag the progress bar to jump to any point in the session.
- **Speed Control**: Adjust playback speed from **0.75x** (slower) to **2x** (faster) to match your pace.
- **Full Screen**: Tap the expand icon or rotate your device to switch to full-screen mode.
- **Audio Quality**: Select your preferred audio quality from the settings menu within the player.

<Tip>
  Use 1.5x speed for revision sessions to cover more ground in less time.
</Tip>

## Downloading for Offline Viewing

You can download recorded sessions to watch later without an internet connection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the download icon">
    On any recorded session, tap the **download icon** next to the video thumbnail or within the player.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the download to complete">
    The video saves directly to your device. Download progress is shown in the notification bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your downloads">
    Open the **Downloads** tab from the bottom navigation to find and play all your saved videos, even without an internet connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Downloaded videos are available for **30 days** or until the batch expires, whichever comes first. After that, you'll need to re-download the session if access is still active.
</Note>

## Bookmarking Key Moments

If you come across an important explanation or concept you want to revisit quickly, use the bookmark feature to mark that exact moment.

Tap the **bookmark icon** during playback to save the current timestamp. All your bookmarks for a session are listed on the session detail page, so you can jump straight back to the moments that matter most without scrubbing through the entire recording.
