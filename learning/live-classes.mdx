Exam Master's live classes let you attend real-time lessons with your teacher from anywhere. You can raise your hand to ask questions, interact with classmates, and pick up where you left off with session recordings.

## Joining a Live Class

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Live Classes section">
    Open the app and go to **Live Classes** from the home screen or bottom navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your scheduled class">
    Find the scheduled class under **Today** or **Upcoming**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Join">
    Tap **Join** — the session will open when the teacher starts it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Allow microphone and camera access if prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're live">
    You're now live! Audio plays automatically; tap the speaker icon to adjust volume.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During a Session

Once you're inside a live class, you have access to a set of interactive tools to make the most of your session:

- **Raise Hand**: Tap the hand icon to signal the teacher you have a question.
- **Chat**: Type messages in the chat panel for the teacher or classmates.
- **Mute/Unmute**: Tap the mic icon to toggle your audio on or off.
- **Full Screen**: Rotate your device or tap the expand icon for a larger view.

<Note>
  You need a stable internet connection (at least 2 Mbps recommended) for the best live class experience.
</Note>

## After the Class

Once a live session ends, a recording is automatically processed and made available in **Recorded Classes** within **30–60 minutes**. You can revisit any part of the lesson at your own pace, rewind key explanations, or catch up if you missed the live session entirely.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't hear the teacher">
    Check that your device volume is turned up and that you haven't muted yourself or the session. Make sure your internet connection is stable. If the issue persists, leave the session and tap **Join** again to rejoin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Join button is greyed out">
    The class hasn't started yet, or you may not be enrolled in that batch. Wait for the teacher to begin the session, or contact your institution if you believe you should have access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Video is lagging">
    Switch to a stronger Wi-Fi connection for a smoother experience. Alternatively, enable **Data Saver** mode in your app settings to reduce bandwidth usage and improve stability.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
