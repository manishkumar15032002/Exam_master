Exam Master keeps all your study resources in one place. Your teachers upload notes, worksheets, formula sheets, and more directly to the platform, so you always have the material you need — whether you're preparing for a test or reviewing a topic between classes.

## Browsing Course Material

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Course Material">
    Tap **Course Material** from the home screen to enter the resource library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a subject">
    Choose the subject you want to study from the list of subjects available in your batch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse by topic">
    Files are organized by topic within each subject. Scroll through the list to find the resource you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Types of Material Available

Your teachers can share a variety of resource types to support your studies:

- **PDF Notes and Handouts** — Detailed notes and summaries from class sessions.
- **Practice Worksheets** — Exercises and problem sets for hands-on practice.
- **Reference Charts and Formulae Sheets** — Quick-reference guides for key concepts, formulas, and data.
- **Previous Year Papers** — Past exam papers to help you familiarize yourself with question patterns.

## Downloading Files

To access any file without an internet connection, download it directly to your device.

Tap the **download icon** next to any file in the Course Material list. The file saves to your device and is accessible offline through the **Downloads** section in the bottom navigation — no internet required once saved.

## Searching Material

Use the **search bar** at the top of the Course Material screen to quickly find what you're looking for. You can search by topic name, subject, or any keyword that appears in the file title.

<Note>
  Material is added by your teachers. If you're expecting a file that isn't showing up, reach out to your teacher or institution directly for assistance.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Organize your downloaded files by subject using the **folder view** in the Downloads tab to keep your resources easy to find before an exam.
</Tip>
