Your Exam Master profile is the central place to manage your personal information, login credentials, and batch enrollments. Keeping your details up to date ensures you receive the right notifications and your institution can reach you when needed.

## Viewing Your Profile

To view your profile at any time, tap the **profile icon** in the top-right corner of any screen. Alternatively, navigate to the **Account** tab from the bottom navigation bar. Your profile displays your name, photo, registered mobile number, email address, and the batches you are currently enrolled in.

## Editing Your Details

You can update your name, profile photo, email address, and other personal information at any time from the Profile settings screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Account → Profile">
    Open the **Account** tab and tap **Profile** to open your profile details page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Edit Profile">
    Tap the **Edit Profile** button at the top of the screen to enter edit mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your information">
    Update your name, profile photo, email address, or any other details as needed. Tap your profile photo to choose a new image from your device gallery or camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Save Changes">
    When you are satisfied with your updates, tap **Save Changes**. Your profile will be updated immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing Your Password

To keep your account secure, you can update your password at any time from the Security settings screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Account → Security">
    Open the **Account** tab and tap **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Change Password">
    Select **Change Password** to open the password update form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Type your existing password in the **Current Password** field to verify your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new password twice">
    Type your new password in the **New Password** field, then retype it in the **Confirm New Password** field to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save** to apply your new password. You may be asked to log in again with your updated credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Enrolled Batches

All the courses and batches you are currently enrolled in are listed under **Account → Profile → Enrolled Batches**. This section shows each batch name, subject, and the institution it belongs to, giving you a quick overview of your active enrollments.

If you need to change a batch enrollment — for example, to switch to a different schedule or course section — contact your institution's support team directly. Batch reassignments are managed by your institution's administrators and cannot be changed from within the app.

<Note>
  Your registered mobile number is your primary login identifier. To change it, contact your institution's support team.
</Note>
