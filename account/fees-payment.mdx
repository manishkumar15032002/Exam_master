Exam Master's built-in payment portal lets you pay your course fees without visiting your institution. All payments are processed through a secure, encrypted gateway, so your financial information stays protected at every step. You can pay using UPI, Debit or Credit Cards, or Net Banking — whichever is most convenient for you.

## Viewing Outstanding Fees

Before making a payment, you can review any amounts currently due on your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Account → Fees">
    Open the **Account** tab and tap **Fees** to navigate to the fees overview screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review pending amounts">
    Any outstanding fee items are listed here along with their **due dates** and associated **course or batch names**. Items overdue will be highlighted to draw your attention.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Making a Payment

Once you have reviewed your outstanding fees, you can complete a payment in just a few steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Account → Fees">
    Open the **Account** tab and tap **Fees**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the fee item">
    Tap the fee item you want to pay to view its full details, including the amount due and the payment deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Pay Now">
    Tap the **Pay Now** button to proceed to the payment screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your payment method">
    Select your preferred payment method from the available options: **UPI**, **Debit/Credit Card**, or **Net Banking**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the checkout">
    Follow the on-screen instructions on the secure checkout screen to authorise and complete your payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your confirmation">
    Once the payment is successful, you will receive a **payment confirmation notification** in the app and your receipt will be saved automatically to your Payment History.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downloading Your Receipt

A receipt is generated automatically for every successful payment and stored in your Payment History.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Account → Fees → Payment History">
    Open the **Account** tab, tap **Fees**, then tap **Payment History**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a transaction">
    Tap any transaction in the list to open its full details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Receipt">
    Tap **Download Receipt** to save a PDF copy of the receipt to your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment History

Every transaction you make through Exam Master is stored permanently under **Account → Fees → Payment History**. Each record includes the payment date, amount paid, course or batch name, and transaction status (Successful, Pending, or Failed). You can refer to this history any time you need to confirm a past payment or retrieve a transaction ID.

<Note>
  If a payment fails but your account was debited, wait 24 hours — refunds are processed automatically by the payment gateway. If the amount is not refunded after 24 hours, contact your institution's support team with your **transaction ID** for assistance.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Save a UPI ID in your payment settings for faster fee submission each term — you won't need to re-enter your details every time a new fee is due.
</Tip>
