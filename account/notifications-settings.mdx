Exam Master sends notifications to help you stay on top of your classes, tests, assignments, and fee deadlines. You have full control over which notifications you receive — you can enable or disable each category individually so your alerts stay relevant and useful without becoming overwhelming.

## Accessing Notification Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Account tab">
    Tap the **Account** tab in the bottom navigation bar to open your account settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Notification Settings">
    Tap **Notification Settings** to open the full list of notification categories and their current status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Notification Categories

Exam Master organises notifications into six categories, each covering a different area of your learning activity. The table below describes what each category includes and its default state.

| Category | What It Covers | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Class Reminders | 15-minute alert before live sessions begin | On |
| Test Alerts | New test assigned, deadline approaching | On |
| Assignment Alerts | New assignment posted, evaluation complete | On |
| Announcements | Messages from teachers and admins | On |
| Fee Reminders | Upcoming fee due dates | On |
| Results | Score published after test evaluation | On |

## Enabling or Disabling Notifications

You can turn any notification category on or off directly from the Notification Settings screen.

Toggle the **switch** next to a category name to enable or disable it. There is no save button — changes take effect immediately and are saved automatically. You can return to this screen at any time to adjust your preferences.

## Re-enabling Device Notifications

If you have previously blocked Exam Master from sending notifications at the operating system level, in-app toggle changes will have no effect until device-level permissions are restored. Follow the steps for your device below.

**Android**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open device Settings">
    Go to your device's **Settings** app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Apps → Exam Master">
    Tap **Apps** (or **Applications**), then find and tap **Exam Master** in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Notifications">
    Tap **Notifications** to open the notification permission screen for Exam Master.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow notifications">
    Toggle **Allow Notifications** (or **Show Notifications**) to the on position. Return to the app and configure your in-app preferences as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**iOS**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open device Settings">
    Go to your device's **Settings** app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Exam Master">
    Scroll down the Settings list and tap **Exam Master**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Notifications">
    Tap **Notifications** to open the notification permission screen for the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Notifications">
    Toggle **Allow Notifications** to the on position. You can also configure alert styles, sounds, and badges from this screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep **Class Reminders** and **Test Alerts** enabled so you never miss a scheduled live session or an upcoming test deadline.
</Tip>
