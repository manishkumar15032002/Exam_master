Exam Master generates detailed performance reports after every test so you can identify your strengths and the areas that need more attention. Reports break down your results by subject, topic, and question type, giving you a clear picture of exactly where to focus your next study session.

## Accessing Reports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Performance">
    Tap **My Performance** from the home screen or your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a test">
    Choose a test from the list to open its full performance report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Report Shows

Each report surfaces six key metrics to give you a well-rounded view of your performance:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Overall Score" icon="star">
    Your total marks and percentage for the test.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subject Breakdown" icon="layer-group">
    Accuracy percentage broken down by subject and individual topic.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Time Analysis" icon="stopwatch">
    Your average time per question compared to the batch average, so you can see where you are spending more or less time than your peers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Peer Rank" icon="trophy">
    Your rank among all students in the same batch who attempted the test.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Correct vs Incorrect" icon="circle-check">
    A clear count of questions you answered correctly, incorrectly, and skipped.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Improvement Trend" icon="arrow-trend-up">
    Your score progression charted across multiple tests so you can see whether your preparation is moving in the right direction.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Creating a Focused Test from Weak Areas

If a report reveals topics where you are consistently losing marks, you do not have to search for those questions manually. Tap **Retry Weak Topics** on the report screen and Exam Master will automatically generate a new test containing only the questions you answered incorrectly. This focused practice helps you target gaps directly without spending time on material you have already mastered.

## Sharing Your Report

To share your results, tap the **share icon** at the top of the report screen. You can export the report as a PDF to save it locally or share it with a parent or guardian directly from the app.

<Tip>
  Review your reports for at least three tests in a row to spot consistent weak areas — these are the topics worth prioritizing in your study schedule.
</Tip>
