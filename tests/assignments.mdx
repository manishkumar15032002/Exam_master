Assignments on Exam Master keep your coursework organized in one place. You can view everything your teacher has assigned, submit your completed work directly through the app, and read the feedback and scores your teacher provides — all without leaving the platform.

## Viewing Your Assignments

To see what you have been assigned, tap **Assignments** from the home screen. You will see a list of all tasks along with their due dates and a status label for each:

- **Pending** — the assignment has not been submitted yet
- **Submitted** — your work has been sent to your teacher and is awaiting review
- **Evaluated** — your teacher has reviewed your submission and left feedback

## Submitting an Assignment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assignment">
    Tap the assignment you want to submit from your Assignments list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the instructions">
    Review the instructions carefully so you understand exactly what your teacher expects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the work">
    Finish the assignment in your notebook or digitally, depending on the task.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your work">
    Tap **Upload** and choose your preferred method:
    - **Camera** — photograph your handwritten or printed work directly with your device camera.
    - **File Picker** — select a PDF or image from your gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an optional note">
    If you want to communicate something to your teacher, type a note in the message field (this step is optional).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Tap **Submit Assignment** to send your work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can re-submit an assignment only if your teacher has enabled revisions for that task.
</Note>

## Checking Evaluation & Feedback

Once your teacher has reviewed your work, the assignment status changes to **Evaluated**. Tap the assignment to open the evaluation screen, where you can see:

- Your score for the assignment
- Written comments from your teacher
- Annotations your teacher has added directly on your submitted work

Reading through this feedback carefully will help you understand what went well and where you can improve on future tasks.

<Tip>
  Photograph your handwritten work in good lighting against a plain background for the clearest images and the most legible submission.
</Tip>
