Practice tests on Exam Master are designed to simulate real exam conditions. You can attempt scheduled tests, review detailed answer explanations, and track your improvement over time.

## Starting a Test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tests section">
    Tap **Tests** from the home screen or bottom navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a test">
    Select a test from the **Available Tests** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the instructions">
    Read the instructions carefully — note the number of questions, time limit, and marking scheme before you begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin the test">
    Tap **Start Test** to begin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test Interface Features

Once your test is underway, a full set of tools is available to help you work efficiently:

- **Question Palette**: See all questions at a glance and mark those you want to review later.
- **Highlight & Strikethrough**: Long-press any answer option to highlight it or strike it through to help narrow down your choices.
- **Bookmark**: Mark a question to revisit before submitting.
- **Timer**: A countdown timer stays visible at the top right so you always know how much time remains.
- **Annotate**: Add a personal note to any question to capture your thinking as you go.

## Submitting Your Test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer remaining questions">
    Work through all questions, or use the Question Palette to check which ones are unanswered before you finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Submit">
    When you are ready, tap **Submit** to end the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your submission">
    A confirmation dialog will appear — tap **Confirm** to finalize your submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your results">
    Your results appear immediately after submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Once you submit a test, you cannot change your answers. Review your responses carefully before tapping Submit.
</Warning>

## Reviewing Your Answers

After submission, tap **View Solutions** to see the correct answer for every question along with a detailed explanation. Working through these explanations is one of the most effective ways to understand mistakes and avoid repeating them.

<Tip>
  Use the Question Palette to tackle easier questions first and return to harder ones before time runs out.
</Tip>
