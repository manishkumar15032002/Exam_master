Your Exam Master account is your gateway to live classes, practice tests, assignments, and everything else the platform has to offer. This page covers how to create and verify your account, log in, keep your profile up to date, and recover access if you ever forget your password.

## Creating Your Account

Before you can access any content, you'll need to register as a student. The sign-up process takes just a couple of minutes and requires nothing more than your name, mobile number, and a password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Exam Master App">
    Launch the Exam Master app on your iOS or Android device. You'll land on the welcome screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Sign Up">
    On the welcome screen, tap the **Sign Up** button to open the registration form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details">
    Fill in your full name, mobile number, and a secure password. Make sure to use a mobile number you actively use — it will be your primary identifier on the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request an OTP">
    Tap **Send OTP**. Exam Master will send a 6-digit one-time password to your registered mobile number via SMS within a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Mobile Number">
    Enter the 6-digit OTP in the verification field. The code is valid for a short window, so enter it promptly. If it expires, tap **Resend OTP** to get a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Registration">
    Tap **Create Account**. Your account is now active and you'll be taken to the batch selection screen to join your enrolled course.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a personal mobile number that you have consistent, long-term access to. Since OTPs and important notifications are sent to this number, switching numbers later may temporarily disrupt your access.
</Tip>

## Logging In

Once your account is created, logging back in is quick. Open the Exam Master app and tap **Log In** on the welcome screen. Enter your registered mobile number and password, then tap **Log In** again to access your dashboard. If your session is still active from a previous visit, you'll be taken directly to your home screen without needing to log in again.

## Updating Your Profile

Keeping your profile accurate helps your instructors and institution identify you correctly, especially for certificates and performance records.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Account Tab">
    Tap the **Account** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open your account settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Your Profile">
    Tap **Profile** at the top of the account screen to open your profile details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Your Information">
    Tap the edit icon or any field you want to update — such as your name, profile photo, or other personal details — and make your changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Changes">
    Tap **Save** to apply your updates. Your profile will reflect the changes immediately across the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resetting Your Password

If you've forgotten your password, you can reset it in a few steps directly from the login screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Forgot Password">
    On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password** below the login form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Registered Mobile Number">
    Type in the mobile number associated with your Exam Master account and tap **Send OTP**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the OTP">
    Check your SMS messages for the 6-digit OTP and enter it in the verification field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a New Password">
    Enter your new password, confirm it, and tap **Reset Password**. You can now log in using your new credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not share your password or OTP with anyone — including people claiming to be Exam Master support staff. Exam Master will never ask for your OTP or password through calls, messages, or emails.
</Warning>
