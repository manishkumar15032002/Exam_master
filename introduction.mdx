## What You Can Do with Exam Master

Exam Master is built around your learning schedule. Here's a quick look at what the platform offers:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Attend Live & Recorded Classes" icon="chalkboard-teacher">
    Join real-time interactive sessions with your teachers. Raise your hand to ask questions, or watch recordings at your own pace — all from one place.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Take Tests & Track Your Performance" icon="chart-bar">
    Practice with subject-wise tests, see your scores instantly, and compare your rank with peers. Detailed reports help you identify where to focus next.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Submit Assignments Online" icon="file-arrow-up">
    Receive assignments from your teachers, complete them, and submit directly through the app. Get feedback without any back-and-forth.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ask Doubts Anytime" icon="circle-question">
    Snap a photo of any question you're stuck on and upload it. Your doubt gets routed to the right teacher for a clear explanation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pay Fees Securely" icon="credit-card">
    Submit course fees online with 100% safe and encrypted payment options — no cash, no queues.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stay Notified" icon="bell">
    Get reminders for upcoming classes, exam dates, new assignments, and special announcements so you never miss a beat.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
