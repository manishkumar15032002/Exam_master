Exam Master's parent dashboard gives you a clear picture of how your child is progressing — attendance records, test scores, assignment submissions, and more — all in one place. You don't need to wait for a report card or a scheduled meeting; the latest data is always available in the app.

## Parent Dashboard Overview

When you open the app and select your child's profile, you'll land on the parent dashboard. Here's what each section shows you:

- **Attendance**: Live class attendance percentage broken down per subject and batch, so you can see exactly which sessions your child has been present for.
- **Test Scores**: A chronological list of all tests your child has taken, including their score, maximum marks, and rank within their batch.
- **Assignments**: The current status of each assignment — **Pending**, **Submitted**, or **Evaluated** — along with any scores awarded.
- **Course Progress**: A visual indicator of how much of your child's enrolled curriculum has been completed to date.
- **Leaderboard**: Your child's current rank among their peers, updated after each test.

## Viewing Detailed Reports

To dig deeper into any individual test result:

1. Tap the test you want to review from the **Test Scores** list on your dashboard.
2. View the subject-wise score breakdown to identify which topics your child performed well in and which need attention.
3. Review the **time analysis** to see how long your child spent on each section.
4. Scroll through the question-level breakdown to see which questions were answered correctly, incorrectly, or skipped.

## Interpreting the Attendance Report

The attendance report uses a simple colour-coding system to help you quickly assess your child's standing:

- 🟢 **Green** — Attendance is above the institution-set threshold. No action needed.
- 🟡 **Yellow** — Attendance is borderline. It's worth encouraging your child to attend upcoming sessions.
- 🔴 **Red** — Attendance has fallen below the institution-set minimum. You may want to speak with the relevant teacher or admin.

## Downloading Reports

You can save or share any report directly from the app. Tap the **share/export icon** (top-right corner) on any report screen to download it as a PDF or share it via your preferred messaging app. This is useful for keeping records or discussing progress with a tutor.

<Tip>
  Review your child's performance reports together to identify areas for improvement and plan study time accordingly.
</Tip>
