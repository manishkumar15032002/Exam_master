As a parent, Exam Master gives you visibility into your child's learning journey — from class attendance to test scores — without disrupting their study time. This guide walks you through setting up a parent account and linking it to your child's profile.

## Downloading the App

Exam Master uses a single app for both students and parents — there's no separate download required. Search for **"Exam Master"** on the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com) or the [Apple App Store](https://www.apple.com/app-store/) and tap **Install**.

## Creating a Parent Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app and tap Sign Up">
    Launch Exam Master after installation and tap **Sign Up** on the welcome screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your role">
    When prompted to choose your role, select **I am a Parent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in your name, mobile number, and create a secure password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your mobile number">
    Enter the one-time password (OTP) sent to your mobile number to confirm your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Tap **Create Account** to complete your registration and access your parent dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Linking to Your Child's Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Add Child">
    After logging in, tap **Add Child** on your home screen to begin the linking process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your child's details">
    Enter your child's registered mobile number or student ID to locate their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for your child's approval">
    Your child will receive a confirmation notification on their device. Ask them to approve the link request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access their profile">
    Once approved, your child's profile will appear on your home screen, giving you full visibility into their activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching Between Children

If you have multiple children enrolled on Exam Master, you don't need separate accounts. Tap the **profile selector** at the top of your home screen to switch between your children's profiles and view each one's individual progress, attendance, and test scores.

<Note>
  You can link up to 5 children's profiles to a single parent account.
</Note>
