Exam Master's built-in chat feature bridges the gap between parents and teachers. You can reach out about your child's attendance, performance, upcoming tests, or any concerns — without waiting for a parent-teacher meeting. All conversations happen inside the app, so everything stays in one place.

## Starting a Conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Messages tab">
    Tap the **Messages** icon in the bottom navigation bar to access your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap New Message">
    Tap **New Message** to start a fresh conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a teacher">
    Browse the list of available teachers, organised by subject and batch, and tap the one you'd like to contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your message">
    Type your message in the text field and tap **Send**. The teacher will be notified of your message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Conversations

Your conversations are automatically organised by teacher, making it easy to refer back to previous exchanges. Whenever a teacher replies to your message, you'll receive a **push notification** so you're never left waiting without knowing. Tap the notification to jump straight to the reply.

## Best Practices

Keeping communication focused and respectful helps ensure you get timely, helpful responses from teachers.

- Keep messages centred on academic topics such as performance, attendance, or upcoming tests.
- Reference specific test names or assignment titles to give the teacher clear context.
- Avoid sending messages late at night — teachers respond during institution working hours.

## Getting Support from Admin

If your question is better directed to the institution's administrative team — such as fee-related queries, schedule changes, or technical issues — use the **Contact Admin** option found under **Account → Help & Support**. This routes your message to the right team rather than the teaching staff.

<Note>
  The teacher chat is only available after your parent account is linked to your child's profile.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use chat to request additional study resources or ask about exam patterns ahead of important tests.
</Tip>
