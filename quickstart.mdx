Exam Master is your all-in-one learning companion — bringing live classes, practice tests, course materials, doubt resolution, assignments, and performance analytics into a single app. This page walks you through everything you need to do to go from a fresh install to attending your very first class or test, all in under five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the App">
    Exam Master is available on both the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. Open the store on your device, search for **"Exam Master"**, and tap **Install** to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Once the app is installed, open it and tap **Sign Up** on the welcome screen. Enter your full name, mobile number, and create a secure password. Tap **Send OTP** — you'll receive a 6-digit verification code via SMS. Enter the code to verify your mobile number and complete your registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join Your Batch">
    After logging in, you'll see a list of available batches and courses linked to your account. Tap your enrolled course to open its home screen, where all your learning content lives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Your Home Screen">
    The home screen gives you a snapshot of everything important — upcoming live classes, recently assigned tests, and quick-action shortcuts. Tap any card to jump straight into that section and start exploring your content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attend Your First Class">
    Head to the **Live Classes** section from the home screen. You'll see all scheduled sessions for your batch. Tap **Join** on any active or upcoming session to enter the live classroom and begin learning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you received an enrollment link or invite code from your institution, tap the link directly or enter the code on the **Join Batch** screen to be automatically added to the correct course and batch.
</Note>
